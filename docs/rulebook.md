# Lord of the Mysteries: Ascension — Rulebook

Two players or four. A free-for-all of potions, Spirituality, and Sanity.

## How you win

- **Last Standing.** Every other contender is at 0 HP.
- **Apotheosis.** Drink the Sequence 0 potion. That player wins immediately.
- **Madness.** If every living player is lost to Sanity, nobody wins.

## The board

Each Beyonder starts at Sequence 9 with 20 HP, 20 Sanity, 0 Spirituality, and 3 cards. HP, Sanity, and Spirituality each cap at 20. Spirituality stays between turns. The deck is 78 cards, one of each. When the last card is drawn, the discard is shuffled into a new deck.

## A turn

1. **Begin.** Gain the Spirituality printed on your current Sequence. If you took no HP since your last Begin, heal 1. If you took no Sanity from an opponent, restore 1 Sanity. From Sequence 6 down, also knit 1 HP. Then draw 1. A charm may add to that draw.
2. **Act.**
   - **Draw once.** The Standard action is one extra draw.
   - **Play any number of cards** while you can pay the printed Spirituality. Commons are 1, rares are 4, the two relic artifacts are 6. Traps spend the Attack action.
   - **Cast your Sequence ability once.** Pay Sanity: 1 at Sequence 9–7, 2 at 6–4, 3 at 3–1. Casting locks Acting for that Sequence.
   - **Attack once.** A bare hand deals no damage. You need a weapon, or an ability that empowers the swing. The attack itself is free.
   - **Drink the potion** when Acting is locked and the recipe is in hand or on your gear. The potion costs no Sanity. Show the cards; they stay where they are, marked, and cannot pay the next potion. Drinking draws 1 and does not spend the Sequence action, so you may cast and drink on the same turn.
3. **End.** Temporary Block and Evade clear. A hand over 7 sets aside cards that are not paying the current potion. Slumber Charm raises the limit by 2.

A late-peak pathway draws 1 more when it casts at Sequence 6 or lower. A mid-peak pathway draws 1 more when it casts at Sequence 6–4. The Mother, at 10 HP or less, mends 2 at Begin, and after she drinks she may take a useful ingredient from the top 3 of the deck.

At 0 Sanity you are no longer a contender: no draw, no cards, no potion. On each of your Begin phases the body deals 1 unblockable HP to every living player, including itself.

Weapons, charms, and artifacts stay equipped. A new one of the same kind replaces the old. You may keep two charms. Traps sit face down and interrupt the printed moment. Equipped cards still pay the potion.

## Peaks

- **Early peak.** The Sun, The Tyrant, The Red Priest, The Hanged Man, The Demoness.
- **Mid peak.** Twilight Giant, The Door, The Darkness, The Death, The Chained, The Abyss.
- **Late peak.** The Fool, The Error, The Visionary, Wheel of Fortune, The Mother, The Moon, The Hermit, The Paragon.
- **Even.** The White Tower, The Black Emperor, The Justiciar.

Early is heaviest at Sequence 9–7, mid at 6–4, late at 3–1. Even holds a similar weight the whole climb. The ability text stays that pathway's own.

## Arcana Concealment

Off unless you check it when you deal. Everyone starts concealed. Playing a rare exposes an opponent and their board. You stay concealed until you attack an exposed player. A concealed player cannot be the target of an Attack. Spells, abilities, and items still do what they say.
